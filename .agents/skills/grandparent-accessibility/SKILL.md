# Grandparent-Friendly Accessibility Standards

Use this skill when designing or altering user interface screens, buttons, overlays, and dialogs.

## 1. Typography & Contrast
* **Font Sizing**: Keep body text above 16sp, and labels/buttons above 18sp.
* **Localization**: Provide clear Tamil translations for all gameplay controls alongside English.
* **Color Contrast**: Enforce WCAG AA or AAA standards. Use dark, rich backgrounds with high-contrast pastel colors for text (like cream, gold, or bright white).

## 2. Touch Target Areas
* **Interactive Elements**: Ensure touch targets are at least **64x64 dp** (exceeding the standard 48dp) to account for shaky fingers.
* **Padding**: Leave ample spacing between buttons to prevent accidental clicks.

## 3. Simplified Turn Flow
* **Visual Guides**: Use glowing pulses or indicators to signal exactly what the user can tap next (e.g. glowing bell pawns when they can move).
* **Automatic Progression**: Auto-skip moves/rolls or advance turns automatically when no legal options exist, reducing cognitive load.
