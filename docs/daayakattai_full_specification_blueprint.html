1. Executive Product Overview & Problem Statement

Traditional South Indian Daayakattai (Dhayam) is an ancient 7×7 grid strategy game played across generations during family gatherings. While modern digital board game apps exist, they consistently fail elderly family members (grandparents) due to:

  • High Friction Onboarding: Email logins, OTPs, passwords, and 6-character room PIN codes that older adults struggle to remember or type.
  • Aggressive Monetization & Dark Patterns: Full-screen interstitial ads with tiny close buttons that trap seniors on unwanted web pages.
  • Disconnected Calling: The separation of gameplay from video/voice calls (requiring multiple devices or clunky picture-in-picture modes).
  • Lack of Visual Guidance: Unclear game states where older eyes cannot distinguish valid moves or understand entry requirements.

The Solution: A dedicated, ad-free, culturally authentic family communication app disguised as a traditional Daayakattai board. With one-tap WhatsApp deep links, call-style incoming push notifications, automatic video anchoring, and permanent one-time family profile setup, grandparents can play with their loved ones with zero technical stress.

3. Daayakattai 7×7 Game Rules & State Machine

The game is played on a 7×7 grid (49 squares) using two 4-sided brass stick dice (*Kattai*) with dot values 0, 1, 2, 3.

Dice Roll Calculation & Bonus Rules

Dice Faces Total Sum Bonus Roll?
0 + 012 (Pannirendu)YES
0 + 11 (Dhayam)YES
0 + 2 / 1 + 12 (Rendu)NO
0 + 3 / 1 + 23 (Moonu)NO
1 + 3 / 2 + 24 (Naangu)NO
2 + 35 (Ainthu)YES
3 + 36 (Aaru)YES

Core Game Mechanics

  • Entry Condition: Coins start in home base. A roll of 1 (Dhayam) is strictly required to move a coin out onto the player's home square.
  • Outer Lap: Coins traverse the outer perimeter (24 squares) counter-clockwise.
  • Malai (Safe Squares): The 9 cross-marked squares (diamond pattern) are safe. Coins on safe squares cannot be cut.
  • Vettu (Capture): Landing on an opponent on an unsafe cell captures their coin and sends it home.
  • Pazham (Inner Track & Goal): A player must execute at least 1 Vettu before their coins can turn into the inner spiral lane toward the center goal (*Pazham*).

7. Testing & Quality Assurance Plan

1. Low-End Hardware & Thermal Testing
Test on entry-level Android devices (2GB–3GB RAM, budget MediaTek/Snapdragon chipsets). Verify that Flutter renders at a stable 60 FPS and that Agora 240p video calling does not cause thermal throttling or rapid battery drain.
2. Network Degradation & Audio Resilience
Simulate throttled 3G networks and 20% packet loss. Ensure the app prioritizes voice audio stream uninterrupted, automatically dropping video frames if necessary without disconnecting the match.
3. Grandparent "10-Second" Usability Benchmark
Conduct live testing with non-tech-savvy seniors over 60 years old. A grandparent should be able to accept an incoming invite and roll the dice within 10 seconds of receiving a notification, without asking for verbal assistance.
